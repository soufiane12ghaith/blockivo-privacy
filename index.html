Privacy Policy for Blockivo

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Welcome to Blockivo. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and trust of our players. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is processed when you install and play Blockivo, how third-party services handle technical data, and what rights you have regarding your device data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Blockivo is designed as an offline-friendly puzzle game. We do not require account registration, login credentials, social media linking, or access to your personal identifiers (such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contacts list).

2. Local Game Storage

Your game progress—including high scores, unlocked levels, collected coins, and audio settings—is stored locally on your device storage. We do not upload or sync this gameplay state to any private external database.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics (Unity Ads)

To support ongoing development and keep the game free to play, Blockivo displays non-intrusive advertisements (such as rewarded videos and interstitial ads) through Unity Ads (provided by Unity Technologies).

Unity Ads automatically collects and processes specific non-personally identifiable technical data to serve, optimize, and measure ad performance, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) or vendor identifiers.
  • Technical Information: Device make and model, operating system version, device language, and screen resolution.
  • Network Data: IP address (used strictly to deduce approximate geographic region such as country or city level).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad delivery metrics, views, clicks, and completion rates of rewarded videos.

You can read more about how Unity handles advertising data by reviewing the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Managing Your Ad Preferences & Opt-Out

You have full control over personalized advertising directly from your device settings:

  • On Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID" to limit personalized tracking.
  • Within Ads: You may tap the Privacy icon (usually marked with an 'i' or AdChoices symbol) shown within Unity Ads to review your choices or opt out of interest-based ads.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Blockivo does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Third-party advertising components inside the game are configured to operate in compliance with child privacy protection regulations (including COPPA and Google Play Families Policies where applicable).

6. Data Security

Because we do not maintain a remote database with personal information, we do not store or transmit your sensitive personal data. Advertising communications between your device and Unity Ads infrastructure are transmitted using industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS / TLS).

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game enhancements, SDK version updates, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
