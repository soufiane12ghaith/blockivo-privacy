Privacy Policy for Blockivo

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Blockivo ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifying information directly (such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses). However, the Game uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain device and usage data.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Unity Ads)

This Game integrates Unity Ads to serve advertisements (interstitial and rewarded video ads). Unity Ads may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to infer approximate country/city location)
  • Device information (hardware model, operating system, screen resolution)
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

For more details on how Unity Technologies processes your data, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Blockivo does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Third-party advertising networks operating in our Game are configured to comply with child privacy regulations and applicable age restrictions.

4. Data Security and Retention

We do not store user data on external personal servers. All ad-related technical data is handled directly and securely by Unity Ads in compliance with industry security standards.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
